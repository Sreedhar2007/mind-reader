Mind Reader AI: Decision Tree & Information Theory

A comprehensive technical breakdown of how artificial intelligence uses Binary Decision Trees, Shannon Information Entropy, and Information Gain to deduce human secret choices.

1. Project Overview & The "Mind Reader" Principle

The Condition Mind Reader creates the illusion of telepathy by asking a sequence of strategic Yes/No questions. The user never reveals, types, or selects their secret number beforehand.

Instead of guessing blindly, the AI uses Binary Decision Tree Traversal. By evaluating candidate conditions (parity, divisibility, inequality, and primality), the AI guarantees halving the search space with every question until only one unique number remains.

3. Information Theory & Shannon Entropy

Claude Shannon’s Information Theory (1948) defines the uncertainty (entropy) of a probability distribution as:

$$H(S) = -\sum_{i=1}^{N} p_i \log_2(p_i) = \log_2(N)$$

Step-by-Step Entropy Reduction

  • Initial State (30 numbers): \(H(S) = \log_2(30) \approx 4.91\text{ bits}\)
  • After Question 1 (15 numbers): \(H(S) = \log_2(15) \approx 3.91\text{ bits}\)
  • After Question 2 (7 numbers): \(H(S) = \log_2(7) \approx 2.81\text{ bits}\)
  • After Question 3 (3 numbers): \(H(S) = \log_2(3) \approx 1.58\text{ bits}\)
  • After Question 4 (1 number): \(H(S) = \log_2(1) = 0.0\text{ bits}\) (Target Deduced!)

Strategic Condition Predicates

  • Parity: x % 2 == 0 (cuts pool exactly in half)
  • Median Partition: x > median (optimal binary cut)
  • Divisibility: x % 3 == 0 and x % 5 == 0
  • Primality: x in primes
  • Perfect Squares: x in {1, 4, 9, 16, 25}

4. College Lecturer Demonstration Script

When presenting this project to your college professor or examiner, use this structured explanation:

  1. Step 1: The Demonstration: Ask the lecturer or a classmate: "Think of any number between 1 and 30 in your head. Do not tell me what it is."
  2. Step 2: Interactive Interrogation: Click "Read My Mind" and read each condition out loud:
    • "Is your secret number even?" -> Click their answer.
    • "Is your secret number greater than 15?" -> Click their answer.
  3. Step 3: Point out the Live Candidate Board: Show how the numbers on screen gray out in real time as the search space shrinks.
  4. Step 4: The Grand Reveal: In just 4 or 5 questions, the AI reveals their exact number!
  5. Step 5: The Academic Explanation: Explain: "This demonstrates a Binary Decision Tree with Shannon Entropy reduction. Because each condition splits the remaining pool approximately 50/50, the time complexity is strictly \(O(\log_2 N)\), meaning 30 numbers require at most 5 questions to isolate."
